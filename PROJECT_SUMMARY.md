## 🎨 Design Specifications Met

### Color Psychology ✅
**Light Mode:**
- Primary: #EBF4FF, #BEDAFF (trust, security)
- Accent: #FFE5D9, #FFCBB3 (friendly, inviting)
- Background: #FDFAF6 (warm, welcoming)
- Green: #F0FFF4, #C6F6D5 (health, tranquility)

**Dark Mode:**
- Primary: #0F172A, #1E293B (trust without harshness)
- Accent: #FF9F80, #FFB89C (warm glow)
- Background: #0A0E14 (soft, not stark)
- Green: #2F855A, #9AE6B4 (health, calm)

### Typography ✅
- **Fonts**: Inter & Nunito Sans
- **Body Size**: 18px minimum
- **Line Height**: 1.6 (readable)
- **Contrast**: AA compliant
- **Hierarchy**: Clear size progression

### Layout ✅
- **Spacing**: Generous, uncluttered
- **Single column**: Mobile
- **Multi-column**: Tablet/Desktop
- **Clean design**: No complexity

### Tone ✅
- **Empathetic**: Understanding user anxiety
- **Supportive**: Encouraging without fear
- **Simple**: No medical jargon
- **Positive**: Focus on benefits
